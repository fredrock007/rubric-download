Transactional Writing Assessment Rubric

CRITERIA A: CONTENT, PLANNING & FORMAT

15 MARKS
LevelMarksDescriptor
Outstanding13–15Outstanding response. Demonstrates insight, maturity, and creativity. Fully developed and coherent ideas. Excellent organization and structure. Excellent understanding and control of format. Appropriate style and tone maintained throughout. Purpose clearly achieved.
Meritorious10–12Very good response. Clear ideas with good development and structure. Well‑organized and coherent. Format fully correct. Tone and style appropriate to purpose. Minor inaccuracies only.
Satisfactory7–9Adequate response showing knowledge and understanding. Some development of ideas, though may lack depth. Organization reasonably clear. Some errors in format or minor lapses in tone/style.
Elementary4–6Some attempt to respond. Limited content or partially irrelevant ideas. Some organization but inconsistent. Format partly correct. Tone/style occasionally inappropriate.
Inadequate0–3Response fails to meet requirements of task. Disorganized or incoherent. Content largely irrelevant or insufficient. Format incorrect or absent. Tone/style unsuitable for purpose.
Subtotal (Criteria A): 15
Annexure B

Transactional Writing Assessment Rubric

CRITERIA B: LANGUAGE, STYLE & EDITING

10 MARKS
LevelMarksDescriptor
Outstanding9–10Excellent tone, register, style, and vocabulary. Language highly appropriate for purpose and audience. Virtually error-free. Excellent sentence construction. Correct spelling and punctuation.
Meritorious7–8Good tone, style, and vocabulary. Language appropriate for purpose and audience. Few minor errors. Fluent and coherent. Good command of grammar and punctuation.
Satisfactory5–6Adequate tone and style, though inconsistent. Reasonable vocabulary and sentence construction. Some grammatical errors but meaning clear. Moderate fluency.
Elementary3–4Limited vocabulary. Tone and register often inappropriate. Frequent grammatical and spelling errors. Errors sometimes obscure meaning.
Inadequate0–2Tone, register, and style completely inappropriate. Vocabulary inadequate. Many serious language errors that obscure meaning.
Subtotal (Criteria B): 10
TOTAL: 25 MARKS (15 + 10)
